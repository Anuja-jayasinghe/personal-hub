A mark for the whole hub

Three marks that stand for you and everything the hub keeps, not only the meters. Each keeps the Switchboard language: one line weight, a terminal dot where a wire ends, and meter red for "now".

A hub is the centre of a wheel and the centre of a network: every spoke is one part of your life the hub keeps (the car, the meters, the bills, the papers). The copper spoke is whatever needs you now, and the mark turns slowly while something is live.

Your initials, a and j, drawn as one continuous wire with a terminal at its end, the way your initials sit in anujajay. The dot of the j is the meter-red lamp, so the most personal part of the mark is also the "now" signal. The most personal of the three.
